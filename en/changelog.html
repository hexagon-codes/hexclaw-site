v0.5.1#

Latest
2026-10-26
  • ImprovedSolve and grade in regular chats: Send text or image questions to a regular assistant without creating a child profile first. When the question is legible but the student’s answer is unclear, it stays ungraded with a separate reference solution.
  • ImprovedTeaching reports and PDFs: Solutions, grading results and guidance for parents come together in one report, with teaching diagrams where needed. Preview, download or print the matching PDF. Verified answers remain available if the explanation fails to generate.
  • ImprovedWeekly and supplementary practice: Generate practice by section, add supplementary sheets and resume interrupted work. Retries keep the original question count; manual practice has its own count and does not use the automatic weekly allowance.
  • ImprovedLearning profiles and progress: Save conflicts preserve your input; backup restoration keeps original images and verified question sources. Progress uses the actual submission time and subject, distinguishing independent work from answers completed with hints.
  • ImprovedPreview and export chat results: Preview complete HTML results, inspect their source and download them. Markdown, JSON and PDF exports retain images uploaded in the conversation.
  • ImprovedResume media tasks: Image, video and speech generation retain execution and delivery states for recovery after interruption. Historical media and local attachments load more reliably, with unknown outcomes shown in their actual state.
  • ImprovedSkills and MCP: Installing, enabling, disabling or removing a Skill or MCP service updates the runtime. Skill bundles can include resources such as templates and scripts.
  • ImprovedConnections and agents: Connections show usage and test status, retain agent bindings after renaming and preserve drafts when templates load. Process labels and Uyghur layouts are more consistent across the three languages.
  • ImprovedLong-term memory: Relevant memories remain available with a Skill attached, scoped by agent, persona or business object. Response conventions are checked each turn. Background refresh keeps the list, search and filters in place.
  • ImprovedLong conversations and sources: Conversation compaction preserves original messages and image references. Search retains source links and supports reading matched pages. Past answers keep the model source used for that turn.

v0.5.0

2026-10-19
  • ImprovedWorkflow canvas: Configure nodes, follow run progress and browse past runs. Import or export workflows, trigger them on a schedule or through a Webhook, and save results to a chosen local directory.
  • ImprovedUnit study materials: Completed materials are saved to the library with a PDF matching the text. Reopen them to read the saved result, check textbook sources and see which teaching Skills were used.
  • ImprovedMath checks and annotated homework: Programmatic checks supplement math solutions, with verified questions reused when source and curriculum conditions match. Correcting a source keeps the original question numbers and corresponding annotated images.
  • ImprovedModels and native reasoning: Model settings distinguish native reasoning support from the thinking switch. Probe reasoning separately and set its effort level. Saving configuration preserves existing credentials and capability declarations.
  • ImprovedConversations and attachments: Improve compatibility with historical attachments and the stability of startup and conversation switching. Tool activity is consolidated, and internal page markers are removed from retrieved knowledge text.
  • ImprovedTeaching messages in DingTalk: Improve emphasis, links and summaries. Long teaching content supports a short summary with a complete PDF for reading on a phone.
  • ImprovedMemory switches apply immediately: The automatic memory extraction and recall switch takes effect after saving, without a restart. Turning it off retains existing memories and manual management.
  • NewHistorical log search: Search disk logs by time, level, source and keyword, with pagination and grouping. Records within the retention period remain available after a restart.

v0.5.0-beta.3

2026-10-04
  • NewK12 homework tutor: Added support for grades 1–6 in math, Chinese, English, science, information technology and art. Included separate assistants, grade and semester settings, math textbook editions and learning profiles for each child.
  • NewTeaching guides: Added parent guides for all six subjects with full solutions, grade-appropriate methods, teaching steps, hints and questions to check understanding.
  • NewPhoto grading: Added grading of completed homework with annotations on the original photo, plus support for full solutions and parent teaching guides for unanswered questions.
  • NewWriting and art feedback: Added revision suggestions and art feedback tied to the original text or visible details, with reasons for each comment.
  • NewLearning records: Added mistake records, learning notes, artwork and progress records. Supported reviews at 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 days, practice generation, printable worksheets and photo grading.
  • NewExports and backups: Added PDF, Word and Markdown learning-profile exports with ZIP attachments, plus K12 backup and restore using .hexbak files.
  • NewDingTalk tutoring: After binding a channel, send homework photos from a phone and receive explanations and annotations in one-to-one private chat.
  • NewScenario packages: Separated scenario-specific views, data and APIs from general Agent capabilities, starting with the K12 package.
  • ImprovedChat and integrations: Updated streaming chat, math rendering, knowledge-base pagination and source filters, MCP restarts and Ollama model management.
  • ImprovedWindows document tools: Aligned Windows packaging with document-rendering dependencies.
